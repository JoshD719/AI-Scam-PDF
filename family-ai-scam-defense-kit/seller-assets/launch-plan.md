# 7-Day Launch Plan

## Day 1 — Problem + Quick Win
Post the 60-second rule. Ask followers to comment “PLAN” if they want a printable checklist. Emphasize calm prevention, not fear.

## Day 2 — Family Code Word
Share why every family should choose a private code word. Show a blurred preview of the wallet card and invite people to set it up tonight.

## Day 3 — Parents and Grandparents
Post the respectful conversation angle: “This is not about taking control. It is about giving everyone a backup plan.” Offer the family pack.

## Day 4 — Fake Text / Bank Alert Demo
Share the safe-link rule: never use unexpected text links; open the official app or type the official site yourself. Point to the fake-text checklist.

## Day 5 — Small Business / Church Angle
Post the vendor payment-change checklist. Message local churches, bookkeepers, contractors, and community groups about the $47 group version.

## Day 6 — Recovery Plan
Share the first-hour recovery checklist: stop contact, save evidence, call provider, change passwords, enable MFA, report. Invite buyers who want a binder-ready plan.

## Day 7 — Feedback + Version 2
Ask buyers which page they printed first. Collect confusion points, missing examples, and group-use requests. Announce planned version 2 improvements.

## Where to Post
Facebook profile and groups, Nextdoor, local business groups, church newsletters, PTA groups, senior-center bulletins, email list, Instagram stories, TikTok/Reels, and neighborhood safety pages.

## What to Say
Lead with the practical outcome: “Your family will have a code word, a before-sending-money checklist, and a recovery plan.” Avoid fear-heavy claims or unsourced statistics.

## How to Offer the Church/Group Version
Pitch it as a one-session handout packet for a family safety night. Include permission boundaries: one group, one event/class, no resale unless separately licensed.

## How to Collect Feedback
Ask three questions: What page did you print first? What felt unclear? What scam message have you seen recently that this should address?

## Version 2 Improvements to Consider
Add bureau-specific credit freeze links after verification, state attorney general resource pages, Spanish-language worksheets, and a facilitator slide deck.
