## Set Up the Family Code Word
Choose a word or short phrase that is not public, not connected to birthdays, pets, schools, addresses, or social media posts. Share it in person or on a known family call. Change it if it is exposed.

## Use the 60-Second Rule
Pause. Verify. Contact directly. Never pay under pressure. Document. Report. Use this rule when anyone asks for money, codes, credentials, bank information, remote access, secrecy, or urgent action.

## Monthly Family Meeting
Spend 20 minutes reviewing suspicious calls and texts, whether the code word is still private, emergency contact updates, account security, and whether an older relative or child needs help.

## If an Active Scam Is Happening
Stop contact. Do not send more money. Save screenshots, numbers, emails, receipts, usernames, and transaction IDs. Contact your bank/payment provider immediately. Change passwords and enable MFA. Report to FTC and FBI IC3 if appropriate. Contact local law enforcement for threats, theft, or active danger.
