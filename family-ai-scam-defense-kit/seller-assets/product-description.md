## Long Product Description
Most families do not need another scary article about scams. They need a simple system they can use when a call, text, invoice, or account alert feels urgent.

**The 2026 Family AI Scam Defense Kit** turns scam prevention into a practical home safety plan. It helps families set up a private code word, teach the 60-second response rule, protect older parents respectfully, prepare kids and teens to ask for help, verify suspicious bank/package/payment texts, and respond quickly if money or identity information has already been exposed.

The kit is written for normal households—not cybersecurity experts. Print it for a binder, put the cards on the refrigerator, keep the wallet card with an older parent, or use the business checklist before approving invoice changes.
