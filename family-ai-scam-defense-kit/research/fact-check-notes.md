# Fact-Check Notes

## Editorial Policy Used in This Draft
- No numerical loss totals, victim counts, or trend percentages are included in the customer-facing PDF draft.
- Claims are phrased as practical safety guidance and tied to official-resource categories in `sources.md` where possible.
- Any future statistical or time-sensitive claim must be verified before sale and added to `sources.md`.

## Claims to Re-Check Before Publishing
- **Voice-clone risk and verification guidance** appears in Sections 1 and 3. Verify current FTC language on harmful voice cloning and defensive verification.
- **Fraud reporting guidance** appears in recovery and resources pages. Confirm FTC ReportFraud and FBI IC3 URLs and names immediately before publishing.
- **Older-adult fraud framing** appears in Section 4 and printables. Confirm CFPB and FDIC/CFPB older-adult resources remain available.
- **MFA, passwords, software updates, and phishing habits** appear in Section 11. Confirm CISA Secure Our World pages remain current.
- **Government, toll, IRS, jury duty, police, court, and benefit message guidance** is intentionally general. If adding agency-specific claims, verify with the relevant official agency page.
- **Credit freeze guidance** appears as a checklist concept only. If adding bureau-specific instructions, verify with Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion official pages.

## Scam-Enabling Content Review
The draft avoids instructions for creating voice clones, deepfakes, phishing pages, evasion tactics, or reusable scam scripts. Examples are defensive and high-level.
